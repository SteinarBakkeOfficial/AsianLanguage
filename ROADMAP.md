# ROADMAP

## Product direction

Build an offline-first iPhone experience where a learner follows one Shared Character from a recognizable origin through historical evolution into modern Chinese, Japanese, and Korean recognition.

## Phases

1. Product foundation — resolved product language and scope
2. App skeleton — five-area root shell
3. Local data layer — bundled corpus and separate local user state
4. Symbol Journey — stage-based navigation, Today endpoint, exact-position resume, completion and recap
5. Discovery — Browse-owned Search, Collections, Learned, Review later, Favorites, and editorial sets
6. Content contract — source-backed stages, variants, regional readings, stage-aware Character structure, publication gate
7. Asset pipeline — bundled renderable Historical Assets and explicit Missing Historical Assets
8. Pilot corpus — Fire, Water, Mountain, Tree, then sourced Horse; 3–5 excellent symbols before scale
9. Design and QA — approved Fire handoff, simulator screenshot comparison, Mac/iPhone interaction checks
10. V1 release — validated corpus, Apple signing, TestFlight/App Store delivery

## Current status

Phases 1–3 and initial discovery/content tooling are implemented. The former six-step lesson and poster-like EvolutionBoardView are transitional and are being replaced by the Symbol Journey architecture. The current 11 records remain draft fixtures.

## V1 to VNext Carryover Register

- Grammar and rule lessons
- Voice/audio
- Tracing/handwriting
- Android
- Sync and deep account systems
- Ads/monetization
- User-created collection folders
- richer historical animation

## Launch targets

The first credible V1 target is about 100 high-quality Shared Characters. The longer-term product vision is 500–1000, after the pilot proves the content and design system.
