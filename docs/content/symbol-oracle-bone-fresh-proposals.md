## Method

Origin already explains what the illustration shows, names the components, explains why they combine to express the meaning, and records sound borrowing or serious uncertainty.

Oracle Bone should therefore compare the selected historical form with the illustration only. It should mention what remains recognizable, what is compressed, distorted, rearranged, or difficult to see. It should not repeat why the components mean the word. Uncertainty is included only when it affects identification.

None of the proposals below is approved yet.
