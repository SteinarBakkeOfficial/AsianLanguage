## Mechanical equivalent/example candidate scan — 2026-10-03 (not the semantic audit)

This is a read-only mechanical screening pass incorporating the previous equivalent/example candidate scan and the Taiwan-divider check. No Symbol content, runtime data, or UI was changed.

### Audit rules applied

- A complete word that directly expresses the lane’s headline meaning is a candidate semantic/native equivalent, not an ordinary example.
- A compound or sentence whose full meaning is narrower, contextual, or extended must remain an example, but its English translation must preserve that specificity.
- A translation equal to only one part of a multi-sense header is a candidate for an overly simplified gloss. It must be checked for whether the full word means that exact sense or a narrower compound meaning.
- A different written graph remains an orthographic variant; a different pronunciation of the same graph remains a reading.
