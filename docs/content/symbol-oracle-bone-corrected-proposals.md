This is the retained Oracle Bone proposal record for the approved V1 caption set. All earlier Oracle proposals are superseded and must not be used as current content.

## Method being applied

The Origin illustration is deliberately made to show what the Oracle Bone form depicts. Origin is where we explain the components, how they combine to express the meaning, and any sound borrowing or uncertainty.

Oracle Bone does not repeat that explanation and does not talk about “matching” or “looking different from” the illustration. Because many selected carved forms are difficult for a modern learner to recognize, the final pass uses a concise visible-form bridge by default. A caption may be empty only when the selected image is genuinely self-explanatory after Origin.

These proposals were approved and applied to the canonical V1 source and generated runtime corpus. Future changes require an explicit Patch 0.1 or later editorial scope.

## Proposed Oracle captions to retain or add

### Day

Current:

> The round sun is already squared off; carving a true circle on bone was difficult. A central mark distinguishes it from an empty enclosure.

Proposed:

> The sun is shown as a square in this form, although it is a circular object.

Reason: the square shape is a meaningful visual change that the learner may not understand from the object itself.

### Look-toward

Current:

> A standing person with an emphasized eye looks into the distance. This precedes the later reorganization of the graph and the addition or replacement of sound-bearing and lunar elements.

Proposed:

> As you can see, the eye is shown clearly inside the person, emphasizing it.

Reason: this points out the important placement without repeating the meaning or later history.

### Sweet

Current:

> A mouth contains a single internal mark/object, indicating something held in the mouth; there is no encoded candy, honey, or separate tongue.

Proposed:

> The small morsel is shown inside the mouth.

Reason: the caption identifies the relationship between the two visible parts without explaining the meaning again.

### Evening

Current:

> The moon shape marks the time around dusk; early forms were not yet consistently separated from the graph for moon.

Proposed:

> Early forms are not yet consistently separated from the related moon form.

Reason: this is a genuine early-form distinction, not a repeated component explanation.

### Few

Current:

> The early few and small forms overlap; their meanings are not yet fully separated.

Proposed:

> Early forms overlap with the related small form.

Reason: the visual and identification uncertainty is useful; the semantic explanation remains in Origin and the later history.

### West

Current:

> One early form resembles a nest or container; another may be a different borrowed form. The sunset explanation is traditional but disputed.

Proposed:

> Early forms vary, and the original object remains uncertain.

Reason: the caption preserves the real uncertainty without inventing a comparison or repeating the Origin interpretation.

### Fragrance

Current:

> No secure Oracle-bone form is used for this museum sequence. V2 does not invent one.

Proposed:

> No secure Oracle Bone form is available for this sequence.

Reason: this is an evidence/status note, not an explanation of the illustration.

### Gather — transmitted variant

Current:

> The transmitted interpretation shows a bird alighting on a tree; exact Oracle identification and multiplicity should be treated cautiously.

Proposed:

> No secure Oracle Bone identification is available for this transmitted form.

Reason: the uncertainty concerns the historical evidence itself and must remain visible.

## All other Oracle captions

For the other 118 symbols, remove the current Oracle explanatory caption and leave the field empty. The Origin illustration and Origin caption already provide the required explanation. Do not replace those captions with phrases such as “matches the illustration,” “looks different,” “remains recognizable,” or “the components remain visible.”
