# Shared CJK 808 Collection Index

Status: discussion research artifact. This is a first-pass editorial sort of the complete 808-character allowed universe. It is not a release plan, a language-frequency ranking, or an etymological classification.

Date: 2026-09-02

## Recommendation

Use 10 semantic collections for discovery and teaching, plus one unnumbered **All Symbols** library containing all 808. The library is the complete corpus boundary; collections are curated views over it. A character receives one primary collection in this index so coverage can be counted without duplication. Later editorial review may add secondary collection membership and cross-cutting lenses.

The 10 collections are:

1. Nature & Cosmos
2. Plants, Animals & Food
3. People, Body & Life
4. Home, Tools & Materials
5. Place, Direction & Movement
6. Time, Number & Measure
7. Family, Society & Institutions
8. Action, Work & Change
9. Mind, Speech & Learning
10. Qualities, Relations & Abstract Ideas

This gives the project a manageable discovery structure without pretending that character meanings fit into mutually exclusive linguistic boxes. A collection is an instructional home, not a claim about how the character was originally formed.

## Secondary membership and lenses

The primary sort is intentionally not the final information model. These relationships should be layered on later:

- **Pictographs:** concrete image-based origins only where historical evidence supports the explanation.
- **Indicators:** marks for quantity, position, or other abstract relations.
- **Associative compounds:** meaning assembled from multiple meaningful parts.
- **Meaning + sound:** semantic and phonetic components, with historical sound evidence kept separate from modern pronunciation.
- **Historical continuity:** oracle-bone, bronze, seal, clerical, regular, or later evidence availability.
- **Component families:** shared parts that become useful in later characters.

For example, a character can live primarily in **Nature & Cosmos**, also appear in a **Pictographs** lens, and belong to a component family. These are different kinds of metadata and should not be collapsed into one category.

## Review cautions

- This first pass optimizes for teachable discovery paths, not equal collection sizes. The counts are not release quotas.
- Some entries are function words, grammatical extensions, names, or historical/cultural vocabulary. They remain in the 808 library because the shared-character boundary is broader than a pictograph curriculum.
- A character's primary collection does not prove that its modern meaning is the meaning represented in an early glyph.
- The full historical evidence register is separate: [`shared-cjk-808-historical-evidence.md`](shared-cjk-808-historical-evidence.md).
