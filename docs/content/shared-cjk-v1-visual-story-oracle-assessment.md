# Shared CJK V1 Visual-Story and Oracle Assessment

Status: historical 148-candidate teaching-order assessment. The current V1 design/content selection is the 126-character complete-evolution subset recorded in [`zdic-v1-complete-manifest.json`](../../content/research/zdic-v1-complete-manifest.json); this document retains the broader ordering and visual-story rationale.

Date: 2026-09-02

## Recommendation

The earlier assessment produced a **148-symbol design sequence**. After the ZDIC-first historical asset intake, the current V1 target is the **126-symbol complete-evolution subset**. This is not a claim that the 126 historical asset packages are commercially cleared or runtime-approved. The selection applies the project's priorities in order:

1. shared identity in the allowed 808;
2. a compelling visual story;
3. preference for simple pictographs and reusable meaningful components;
4. preference for an Oracle Bone indexed form;
5. avoidance of formations whose components mainly provide sound; and
6. enough breadth for a coherent museum rather than only a list of isolated pictures.

The first 50 of the earlier 148-rank sequence are the entrance to the museum. They are intentionally simple and component-rich. Later ranks show how those visual ideas combine, shift, and become more abstract. The current V1 asset target is the 126-character subset with complete ZDIC stage selections; historical asset approval remains a separate row-level gate. If a V1 row ultimately fails specialist, language, usage, or rights review, replace it from the same 808-based process rather than weakening the visual-story rule.

## Evidence-based screen

The 808-character boundary and the formation data were screened as follows:

| Screen | Result | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Allowed shared-character universe | 808 | Hard identity boundary for the product corpus. |
| Non-phono primary formation labels | 383 | Pictographic, indicative, or ideographic screening pool. |
| Non-phono + EVOBC Oracle Bone index | 284 | Strongest broad machine-indexed pool for this museum. |
| Earlier visual-story design reference | 148 | Curated for visual teaching and sequence, not selected to hit a quota. |
| Current ZDIC-complete V1 reference | 126 | Only records with selected Oracle Bone, Bronze, Small Seal, and Clerical images. |
| Recommended set labelled pictographic | 78 | Mostly direct visual referents; still needs row-level review. |
| Recommended set labelled indicative | 3 | `上`, `下`, and `一`-type spatial/marking lessons. |
| Recommended set labelled ideographic | 66 | Meaningful combinations or conceptual constructions. |
| Direct visual exception labelled phono-semantic | 1 | `言`: direct tongue/speech pictograph evidence outweighs the aggregate label for teaching review, but conflict must be recorded. |
| Earlier 148-candidate reference with EVOBC OBC index | 147/148 | `手` was retained as the sole foundational Bronze-earliest exception despite no EVOBC OBC hit in that pass. |

The formation labels come from the open [Hanzi/Kanji Etymology Dictionary](https://github.com/lbm364dl/hanzi-etymology-dict), which aggregates sources and preserves conflicts. They are a screening aid, not specialist adjudication. The historical index comes from the project's normalized comparison with [EVOBC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.12467). EVOBC covers Oracle Bone, Bronze, Seal, Spring and Autumn, Warring States, and Clerical stages, but an indexed record does not by itself prove the exact glyph interpretation or licensing path.

The distinction matters: an Oracle Bone index record establishes historical research availability, not automatically a final image file, exact glyph interpretation, or a licensing path. In the locked set, `手` is the deliberate exception: an independent Dong Chinese record supplies Bronze, Seal, Clerical, and Regular stages, but the current EVOBC comparison did not return an Oracle Bone hit. `看` is not locked because the current independent evidence presents it as a phono-semantic compound with only a Seal-stage entry, which is too weak for this visual-first museum gate.

## Ordered museum sequence

The order is the instructional rank. Read each line from left to right; the numbers are the exact `teaching_order` values.

### Ranks 1–50: foundational pictographs and reusable components

These should form the learner's first visual vocabulary. They are concrete referents, body forms, natural forms, animals, tools, and materials that can later reappear inside compounds.

```text
1 人   2 女   3 子   4 大   5 小   6 口   7 目   8 耳   9 手   10 足
11 身  12 首  13 面  14 舌  15 肉  16 心  17 言  18 自  19 火  20 水
21 山  22 木  23 土  24 石  25 川  26 日  27 月  28 天  29 雨  30 田
31 井  32 泉  33 牛  34 犬  35 羊  36 马  37 鱼  38 鸟  39 虎  40 角
41 竹  42 米  43 豆  44 衣  45 皮  46 玉  47 王  48 刀  49 弓  50 车
```

`言` is placed after `口` and `舌` because its visual lesson is speech/tongue, even though the aggregate formation field calls it phono-semantic. It must be presented as a reviewed exception, not silently treated as sound-free.

### Ranks 51–84: objects, signs, position, quantity, and contrast

These extend the component vocabulary into marks, spatial relations, directions, quantities, and visible contrasts. They should follow the first concrete forms rather than compete with them at the museum entrance.

```text
51 门  52 户  53 贝  54 片  55 册  56 示  57 工  58 力  59 夕  60 由
61 申  62 云  63 西  64 南  65 北  66 东  67 年  68 老  69 白  70 黑
71 赤  72 黄  73 长  74 高  75 多  76 少  77 正  78 上  79 下  80 中
81 一   82 十  83 二   84 三
```

The number and position lessons are not filler. They show the learner that the writing system did not begin and end with drawings of physical objects. They are early structural steps toward abstraction.

### Ranks 85–95: movement and perception bridges

These should be introduced once the learner has the body, foot, direction, and visual vocabulary needed to understand them.

```text
85 出  86 入  87 来  88 行  89 走  90 见  91 立  92 止  93 生  94 央  95 林
```

The previously considered `看` candidate is intentionally omitted from the locked sequence. It remains a possible later research candidate only if a stronger early visual and historical path is established.

### Ranks 96–148: meaningful compounds and advanced visual stories

These are not the entrance examples. They show how known pictographs and indicators combine into richer meanings. They are especially valuable when the parts remain semantically meaningful and the lesson can distinguish historical evidence from later scholarly interpretation.

```text
96 光   97 明   98 休   99 好  100 男  101 母  102 兄  103 友  104 公  105 民
106 兵  107 典  108 令  109 先  110 及  111 从  112 取  113 采  114 反  115 交
116 同  117 合  118 各  119 告  120 向  121 望  122 步  123 分  124 利  125 前
126 後  127 集  128 得  129 益  130 甘  131 争  132 祭  133 武  134 族  135 旅
136 夏  137 冬  138 美  139 宗  140 守  141 官  142 宿  143 妻  144 祝  145 香
146 古  147 吉  148 品
```

Key dependency examples:

- `林` follows `木`: two trees make a forest/grove lesson.
- `光` follows `火` and the person/kneeling-person concept: fire above a person. This is an advanced meaningful compound, not a replacement for the simple pictograph foundation.
- `明` follows `日` and `月`, with a learner-facing caveat that historical forms and later explanations include a window-and-moon interpretation; it should not be reduced to an unqualified “sun plus moon” claim.
- `休` follows `人` and `木`: a person leaning against a tree to rest.
- `好` follows `女` and `子`: a meaningful relational compound, with cultural interpretation handled carefully.
- `祭` follows `手`, `肉`, and `示`: an offering action over an altar.
- `集` follows the bird and tree concepts: birds gathered on a tree.
- `美` follows `羊` and `大`: a meaningful historical explanation that requires careful editorial wording.

## What was deliberately excluded

The following exclusions are intentional:

- `花`: its current source-backed explanation is semantic `艹` plus phonetic `化`, and it lacks the reviewed historical visual path needed for the first museum.
- `家`: historically important, but the current explanation is primarily roof semantic plus pig phonetic; it is better saved for a later formation lesson if a stronger visual treatment is developed.
- `字`, `问`, `闻`, and `念`: useful language-learning characters, but their current evidence is more sound-driven or later/abstract than the visual-first V1 requires.
- `学`: potentially meaningful as a historical learning scene, but its current formation and stage evidence require a separate specialist pass; it should not displace clearer foundational material.
- `矢`: an excellent pictograph of an arrow, but it is outside the project's verified shared 808 universe and therefore cannot be a product candidate under the current hard identity rule. It may appear only as a historical component explanation inside another approved character if that treatment is justified.

This does not say that excluded characters are unimportant or have no history. It says they do not currently earn a place in the first visual-story curriculum under the project's stated priorities.

Use the existing ten semantic collections as discovery homes, without forcing equal counts. The sequence should cut across collections so that the learner builds components in a useful order. The cross-cutting lenses remain:

- Pictographs — strongest in ranks 1–50;
- Indicators and spatial signs — concentrated in ranks 51–84;
- Meaningful compounds — concentrated in ranks 96–148;
- Components — shows reuse across the sequence;
- Historical Continuity — highlights which stages are actually source-backed.

The primary assignment above is a design reference, not an implementation schema lock. A character may have one primary collection and multiple lenses; collection membership must not alter its teaching order.

## Collection artwork and current prototype dimensions

For the upcoming illustration brief, request ten distinct editorial collection covers—one per semantic collection—and do not reuse a symbol's educational origin illustration as an unrelated collection cover. The cover should communicate the collection's visual world while remaining compatible with the approved restrained editorial reference. The complete symbol library remains a separate Browse destination; it is not counted as a semantic collection.

The Collections screen now uses the supplied full horizontal panels as the collection buttons. Its measured layout targets are:

| Surface | Current prototype size | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Collections collection button | **Full available width × native panel ratio** | The complete horizontal panel is tappable; current source crops are approximately 4.1:1–4.7:1. |
| Collections button at 361 pt content width | **Approximately 77–88 pt high** | Height varies slightly because the supplied panel crops are not identical in pixel height. |
| Browse collection-preview artwork | **96 × 72 pt** | Preview artwork used on the Browse-side collection link/card. |
| Browse collection link row | **56 pt minimum height** | Full tappable Browse link row. |
| Primary action button | **44 pt minimum height** | Current shared button target, not collection artwork. |

These are SwiftUI layout points in the current prototype, not final raster dimensions. For ChatGPT artwork, use a larger source canvas with the same horizontal composition and preserve the complete panel; do not generate square artwork. The collection-cover brief should specify both the source canvas and crop behavior, rather than treating a display point size as an image-generation resolution.

## Required next review

Before locking runtime content, create one reviewed row for each of the 126 current V1 records with:

- the exact visual story and a learner-safe wording;
- prerequisite concepts and component references;
- formation classification with conflicting source claims preserved;
- exact Oracle Bone/Bronze/Seal/Clerical assets and provenance;
- independent Xiaoxuetang or paleographic confirmation where EVOBC is missing or disputed;
- Japanese Kanji and Korean Hanja identity evidence;
- Mandarin/Traditional Chinese, Japanese, and Korean usage examples;
- illustration brief separate from the historical glyph asset; and
- specialist, native-speaker, rights, and editorial status.

The 126 complete-evolution identities are the current V1 design/content target. Runtime content remains pending until each row has a reviewed visual story, modern-language record, rights status, and asset record. “OBC-indexed” and “ZDIC image downloaded” are research statuses, not permission to fabricate or ship a historical glyph.

No generated illustration may fill a missing historical stage. The educational illustration should make the visual story understandable, while the historical glyph remains a separately sourced evidence asset.
