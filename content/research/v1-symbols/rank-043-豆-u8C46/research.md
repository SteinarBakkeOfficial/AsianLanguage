## Current visual-story evidence

An old serving dish 口 on a stand with a lid 一 / Pictograph of a stemmed bowl. Current meaning is a phonetic loan. / 古食肉器也。从口，象形。凡豆之屬皆从豆。 / Pictogram (象形) – some kind of container. Borrowed phonetically for the plant name. Displaced Old Chinese 菽 (shū). Unrelated to the bottom part of 豊.
Derived from 頭 (OC *doː), ultimately from Proto-Sino-Tibetan *du (“head”) (STEDT).
"Soybean > bean" is possibly the same word as "round vessel" (Unger, 1984)
