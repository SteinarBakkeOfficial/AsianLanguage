# Educational origin illustration brief: 言

Use case: scientific-educational
Asset type: educational origin reconstruction for the Script Roots Symbol Journey

## Primary request

Illustrate the concrete object, action, body form, or meaningful scene behind the earliest defensible story for 言. The composition must be understandable as a real-world referent first, then visually echo the silhouette, arrangement, or meaningful parts documented in the historical evidence. The learner should be able to compare this illustration with the separately displayed earliest glyph and understand the proposed transition without the illustration pretending to be the glyph.

## Source cues

A tongue sticking out of a mouth 口 / Pictograph of a tongue sticking out of a mouth. In old scripts written the same as 舌 but with an extra line. / 直言曰言，論難曰語。从口䇂聲。凡言之屬皆从言。 / Ideogram (指事) – Early oracle bone script: 口 (“mouth”) + ▽ (possibly original form of 弋, marking location). Later: 舌 (“tongue”) (mouth with a snake's tongue) + ▽, sharing strokes at the tip. By the Spring and Autumn and Warring States periods, a horizontal stroke was added above to indicate the source of speech or the words spoken.
See also 音.
Shuowen erroneously analyzes the glyph as a phono-semantic compound (形聲 /形声): phonetic 䇂 (qiān) + semantic 口 (“mouth”).
According to Schuessler (2007: 74) this word is derived from 語 (OC *ŋaʔ, “to speak”) + -n nominal suffix.

## Composition constraints

- Keep the meaningful object or relationship visually dominant and uncluttered.
- If the formation is multi-component, show each meaning-bearing component in a single coherent scene.
- Do not invent a component meaning when the source marks it as phonetic.
- Do not draw Oracle Bone, Bronze, Seal, Clerical, or Regular glyphs inside the illustration. Those remain separate historical evidence assets.
- Use the approved warm paper, ink, muted mineral, and restrained editorial museum direction used by the existing 11-symbol concept art.
- No modern text, labels, watermark, decorative border, or square icon treatment.
