# Educational origin illustration brief: 由

Use case: scientific-educational
Asset type: educational origin reconstruction for the Script Roots Symbol Journey

## Primary request

Illustrate the concrete object, action, body form, or meaningful scene behind the earliest defensible story for 由. The composition must be understandable as a real-world referent first, then visually echo the silhouette, arrangement, or meaningful parts documented in the historical evidence. The learner should be able to compare this illustration with the separately displayed earliest glyph and understand the proposed transition without the illustration pretending to be the glyph.

## Source cues

Road 丨 leading to the farm 田 / Origin unclear. / Pictogram (象形) – a fruit (specifically a pomelo).
Wu and Shi (2022) notes that 由 was originally a component extracted from the oracle bone script of 柚 (“pomelo”), where it depicted the fruit itself. It was later used exclusively for phonetic loans, fully differentiating from 柚.
Traditional analysis in Shuowen interprets it as a container with a needle (十, original form of 針). Alternatively, Tang Lan (唐蘭) interpreted it as the pictogram of a helmet (original character of 冑).

## Composition constraints

- Keep the meaningful object or relationship visually dominant and uncluttered.
- If the formation is multi-component, show each meaning-bearing component in a single coherent scene.
- Do not invent a component meaning when the source marks it as phonetic.
- Do not draw Oracle Bone, Bronze, Seal, Clerical, or Regular glyphs inside the illustration. Those remain separate historical evidence assets.
- Use the approved warm paper, ink, muted mineral, and restrained editorial museum direction used by the existing 11-symbol concept art.
- No modern text, labels, watermark, decorative border, or square icon treatment.
