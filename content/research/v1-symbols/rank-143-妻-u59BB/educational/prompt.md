# Educational origin illustration brief: 妻

Use case: scientific-educational
Asset type: educational origin reconstruction for the Script Roots Symbol Journey

## Primary request

Illustrate the concrete object, action, body form, or meaningful scene behind the earliest defensible story for 妻. The composition must be understandable as a real-world referent first, then visually echo the silhouette, arrangement, or meaningful parts documented in the historical evidence. The learner should be able to compare this illustration with the separately displayed earliest glyph and understand the proposed transition without the illustration pretending to be the glyph.

## Source cues

A woman 女 holding a broom 十 in her hand 彐 / Pictograph of a hand grabbing a woman's hair. In ancient China, women of marriagable age wore hairpins (compare with 每). / 婦與夫齊者也。从女从屮从又。又，持事，妻職也。 / Ideogrammic compound (會意 /会意): 肀 (“hand grabbing hair”) + 女 (“woman”) ― a man grabbing a woman's hair to show ownership (marriage by capture).
Thought to be cognate to 齊 (OC *dzêi, “same, equal (adj.), in line”) (SWJZhttps://ctext.org/shuo-wen-jie-zi/nv-bu#n34692; Karlgren, 1956) & interpreted as "an equal to her husband"; yet this is unlikely considering the realities of ancient societies (Schuessler, 2007).
Schuessler instead proposes Austroasiatic etymology: he reconstructs 妻 (OC *tshə̂i) < *k-sə̂i and compares it to Middle Khmer [script needed] (kansai, “wife”) (whence Khmer កន្សៃ (kɑɑkɑnsay, “wife”)) and Khmer [script needed] (*khsay, “be female”) with *ka(n)- being a female marker (ibid.).

## Composition constraints

- Keep the meaningful object or relationship visually dominant and uncluttered.
- If the formation is multi-component, show each meaning-bearing component in a single coherent scene.
- Do not invent a component meaning when the source marks it as phonetic.
- Do not draw Oracle Bone, Bronze, Seal, Clerical, or Regular glyphs inside the illustration. Those remain separate historical evidence assets.
- Use the approved warm paper, ink, muted mineral, and restrained editorial museum direction used by the existing 11-symbol concept art.
- No modern text, labels, watermark, decorative border, or square icon treatment.
