## Current visual-story evidence

Grains of rice / Pictograph of grains of rice. / 粟實也。象禾實之形。凡米之屬皆从米。 / Pictogram (象形) – rice kernels without husk. The character 粟, by contrast, refers to rice kernels with husk. According to Li Xiaoding (李孝定), the horizontal stroke is a bamboo sieve (籭 or 篩子). See also the bottom component of 暴 in its original form.
Unrelated to 木, 禾, and 来.
From Proto-Sino-Tibetan *mej (“grain”). Cognate with Garo mi (“rice”), Atong (India) mai (“rice”), Jingpho ma (“rice; paddy”), Rabha মাই (mai), Dimasa mai. / Short for 米突 (mǐtū), from French mètre.
