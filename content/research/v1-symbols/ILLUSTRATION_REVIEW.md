# V1 Illustration Review Index

These are draft educational concept images for manual review. Each image depicts the concrete referent or meaningful scene first, using the warm paper and charcoal/mineral wash direction established by the pilot. They are intended to help a learner compare an object or action with a separately displayed, source-backed historical form.

They are not historical reconstructions of glyph shapes. No generated image may be used as Oracle Bone, Bronze, Seal, Clerical, or Regular evidence. Historical evidence remains in each symbol folder under `historical/`, with provenance in `historical-references.json` and `sources.json`.

## Generation rule

For every new asset, the image must show one concrete referent or one meaningful visual relationship. It must not contain Chinese characters, invented glyphs, artifact plates, labels, timelines, or modern forms. Historical forms are copied only from source-backed files and shown separately.
