## Current visual-story evidence

Birds 隹 flocking on a tree 木 / Depicts a bird (隹) gathering wood (木) to build a nest. / Ideogrammic compound (會意 /会意): 隹 (“bird”) + 木 (“tree”).
The variant 雧 (jí) shows three birds instead.
Cognate with 雜 (OC *zuːb, “mixed”) (Karlgren, 1956). Compare Khmer ជួប (cuəp, “to meet”) (Schuessler, 2007).
