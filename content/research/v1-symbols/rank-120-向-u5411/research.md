## Current visual-story evidence

Depicts a mouth (口) in a closed space (宀) making an echo sound. Based on the original meaning "echo; sound", now written as 響. The current meaning is a phonetic loan. / 北出牖也。从宀从口。《詩》曰：“塞向墐戶。” / Ideogrammic compound (會意 /会意): 宀 (“house”) + 口 (“mouth”), echos in a place. The original form of 響 (OC *qʰaŋʔ). It was borrowed to represent 𠨍 (嚮) later. The mouth does not represent an opening as in 高 and 谷. Compare 冋.
