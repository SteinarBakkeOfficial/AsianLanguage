# Educational origin illustration brief: 立

Use case: scientific-educational
Asset type: educational origin reconstruction for the Script Roots Symbol Journey

## Primary request

Illustrate the concrete object, action, body form, or meaningful scene behind the earliest defensible story for 立. The composition must be understandable as a real-world referent first, then visually echo the silhouette, arrangement, or meaningful parts documented in the historical evidence. The learner should be able to compare this illustration with the separately displayed earliest glyph and understand the proposed transition without the illustration pretending to be the glyph.

## Source cues

A man standing on the ground 一 / Pictograph of a person standing on the ground. / 住也。从大立一之上。凡立之屬皆从立。 / Pictogram (象形) – a person standing on the ground; compare 大 and 文. This characters is also a contracted form of 辛 on top of 妾 and 童. Unrelated to 音.
From Proto-Sino-Tibetan *kV-r(j)əp (“to stand”). Cognate with 位 (OC *ɢʷrɯbs). / From English litre.

- Keep the meaningful object or relationship visually dominant and uncluttered.
- If the formation is multi-component, show each meaning-bearing component in a single coherent scene.
- Do not invent a component meaning when the source marks it as phonetic.
- Do not draw Oracle Bone, Bronze, Seal, Clerical, or Regular glyphs inside the illustration. Those remain separate historical evidence assets.
- Use the approved warm paper, ink, muted mineral, and restrained editorial museum direction used by the existing 11-symbol concept art.
- No modern text, labels, watermark, decorative border, or square icon treatment.
