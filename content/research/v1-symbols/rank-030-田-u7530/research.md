## Current visual-story evidence

The plots of a rice paddy / Pictograph of a field. / 陳也。樹穀曰田。象四囗。十，阡陌之制也。凡田之屬皆从田。 / Pictogram (象形) – pictographic representation of a field divided into four sections. See the original version of 甫 and 里.
Sometimes it means mask. See 異 and 鬼. Unrelated to 思, 黄, 果, 由, 甲, and perhaps 番.
From Proto-Sino-Tibetan *mV-liŋ (“field”). Cognate with Tibetan ཞིང (zhing, “field; realm”), Jingpho maling (mă³¹ liŋ³³, “forest”). Compare Proto-Hmong-Mien *ljiŋ (“field”). / The Min native word for “paddy field; field”.
Etymology unknown. Chinese scholars identify 塍 (OC *ɦljɯŋ, “raised path between fields”) as the etymological character (本字), although Norman proposes that this is related to 層 (OC *zɯːŋ, “layer”), reflecting the terraced fields commonly found in Fujian (Schuessler, 2007). Compare also 㽪 (“wet field”).
