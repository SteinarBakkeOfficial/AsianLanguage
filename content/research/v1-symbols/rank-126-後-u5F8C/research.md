## Current visual-story evidence

One step 彳 coming behind another 夂; 幺 provides the pronunciation / Phonosemantic compound. 幺 represents the sound, and 彳 (walk) and 夂 (foot) are used together to depict following behind someone. / 遲也。从彳、幺、夊者，後也。 / In oracle bone script, ideogrammic compound (會意 /会意): 幺 (“thread”) + 夊 (“foot”) – a foot bound by a rope, thus lagging behind. The component 彳 was added afterwards to emphasize this action.
Note that the simplified form 后 is also a traditional character on its own, with a different meaning.
From Proto-Sino-Tibetan *hwak (“below”) (STEDT). Cognate with Tibetan འོག ('og, “below”), Burmese အောက် (auk, “under; below”) (from Proto-Lolo-Burmese *ʔok (“lower side; below”)).
