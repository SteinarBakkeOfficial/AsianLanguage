## Current visual-story evidence

The leg 口 above the foot / Pictograph of a foot attached to a leg. / 人之足也。在下。从止、口。凡足之屬皆从足。 / Pictogram (象形) of a leg; the pictogram is similar to ⽦. See also 𠯣, which points to the fact that 之 is the pictogram of a footprint.
Uncertain. Compare Proto-Mon-Khmer *ɟuŋ ~ *ɟuəŋ ~ *ɟəŋ (“leg, foot”), whence Mon ဇိုၚ် (cf. Schuessler, 2007). / Probably Sino-Tibetan; compare Tibetan ཆོག (chog, “to be sufficient”) (Coblin, 1986).
