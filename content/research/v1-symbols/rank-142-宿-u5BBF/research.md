## Current visual-story evidence

A person 亻 sleeping in a bed 百 under a roof 宀 / 止也。从宀𠈇聲。𠈇，古文夙。 / In the oracle bone script, an ideogrammic compound (會意 /会意): 人 (“person”) + ⿴囗⿱人仌 (“bamboo mat”) – a person resting on a mat. Sometimes a house (宀) was added.
Later the pictogram of the bamboo mat became 㐁 or 𠀬, and eventually came to be written 百.
Contrast 寒.
; “To stay overnight; lodge > night; mansion of the zodiac [i.e. the Sun's lodging stations]”
