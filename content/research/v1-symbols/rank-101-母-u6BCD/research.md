## Current visual-story evidence

A mother's breasts / Pictograph of a woman kneeling, with two dots representing the two nipples that mothers use to feed their young. / 牧也。从女，象褱子形。一曰象乳子也。 / Differentiated form of 女 with the addition of distinguishing dots. In the early oracle bone texts both words 女 (OC *naʔ, *nas, “woman”) and 母 were written as 女 (Yao, 1989, Liu, 2011, Huang, 2014). Part of this character appears in 乳.
From Proto-Sino-Tibetan *məʔ (“mother”).
