## Current visual-story evidence

Depicts a boat (舟) moving (止) forward (前). / 不行而進謂之歬。从止在舟上。 / Originally 歬, an ideogrammic compound (會意 /会意): 止 (“foot”) + 舟 (“boat”) – a foot on a boat moving forward.
In the current form, 止 is simplified to 䒑 and 舟 to 月. The current form with an additional 刀 (“knife”) (𠝣 > 𣦃 > 前) is the original form of 剪 (OC *ʔslenʔ, “to cut”).
Unrelated to 俞.
