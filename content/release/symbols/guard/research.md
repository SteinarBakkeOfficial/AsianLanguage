## Current visual-story evidence

Keeping something 寸 within one's walls 宀 / Depicts a hand (寸) guarding a building (宀). / 守官也。从宀从寸。寺府之事者。从寸。寸，法度也。 / Ideogrammic compound (會意 /会意): 宀 (“roof; building”) + 寸 (“hand”); to guard, keep, or defend a place (e.g., a building whose roof is visible on top of the character). It's possible that 寸 (original form of 肘 (OC *tkuʔ)) is also a phonetic component. A more conservative version is 㝊, in which 又 is not a phonetic component.
