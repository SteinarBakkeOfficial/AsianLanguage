## Current visual-story evidence

Selected production evidence: 母 is the 女-type kneeling/side-view female pictograph with two added chest-area marks traditionally interpreted as breasts, distinguishing motherhood. Early Oracle usage sometimes used 女 and 母 interchangeably; the marks are graphically significant in the selected specimen but should not become floating dots or symbols in the standalone artwork. Part of this character appears in 乳.
From Proto-Sino-Tibetan *məʔ (“mother”).
