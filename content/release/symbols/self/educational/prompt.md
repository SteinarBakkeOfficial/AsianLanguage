## Source cues

A nose; in China, people refer to themselves by pointing to their noses / Pictograph of a nose. Original form of 鼻 (nose). In China, when people point to themselves, they typically point to their nose. / 鼻也。象鼻形。凡自之屬皆从自。 / Pictogram (象形): a nose — original form of 鼻 (OC *blids, “nose”). Later phonetically borrowed as an instance of jiajie (假借) for 自 (OC *ɦljids, “oneself; from”).
Folk etymology considers the meaning to arise from a habit of pointing at one’s nose to indicate oneself.
