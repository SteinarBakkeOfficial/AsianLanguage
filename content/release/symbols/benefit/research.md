## Current visual-story evidence

Harvesting 刂 grain 禾 / Depicts harvesting grain (禾) with a blade (刂). Harvest was the main source of profit in agricultural societies. / 銛也。从刀。和然後利，从和省。《易》曰：“利者，義之和也。” / Ideogrammic compound (會意 /会意): 禾 (“grain”) + 刀 (“knife”) – to reap grain (禾) with a knife (刀). See also the variant 𥝢, which appears on top of the character 黎.
From Proto-Sino-Tibetan *ri:t (“to reap, scrape, shave, cut, sever”) (STEDT, Schuessler, 2007); cognate to Mizo rîit (“to scrap with a hoe”), Gurung [script needed] (wriqba, “to scratch”), Burmese ရိတ် (rit, “to cut, reap, mow shave”).
