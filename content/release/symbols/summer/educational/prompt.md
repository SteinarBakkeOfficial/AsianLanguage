# Educational origin illustration brief: 夏

Use case: scientific-educational
Asset type: educational origin reconstruction for the Script Roots Symbol Journey

## Primary request

Illustrate the concrete object, action, body form, or meaningful scene behind the earliest defensible story for 夏. The composition must be understandable as a real-world referent first, then visually echo the silhouette, arrangement, or meaningful parts documented in the historical evidence. The learner should be able to compare this illustration with the separately displayed earliest glyph and understand the proposed transition without the illustration pretending to be the glyph.

## Source cues

Pictograph of a person, represented by their head (頁) and feet (夂), being heated underneath the sun (日). The sun component was later omitted for brevity during the Warring States period. / 中國之人也。从夊从頁从𦥑。𦥑，兩手；夊，兩足也。 / Some scholars identify an oracle bone character ideogrammic compound (會意 /会意): 日 (“sun”) + 頁 (“head; man”) as this character – a man under the scorching sun; summer. However, it only appears as a diviner's name, making the identification uncertain.
In Spring and Autumn period bronze inscriptions, various variants appeared. 止 (“foot”) was often added below the man, and in some forms 日 was removed while 𦥑 (“two hands”) was added. The seal script inherits from the latter form, with 止 replaced by the related 夊.
Warring States period forms also varied. The bamboo and silk script above shows a common form: 日 + 止 + 頁. The "ancient script" (古文) in Shuowen (labelled as large seal script) has deviated significantly, with 止 becoming 足 and the top part possibly a corruption of 頁.
The current form derives from the seal script, with 𦥑 and the legs of 頁 removed. Unrelated to 复.
