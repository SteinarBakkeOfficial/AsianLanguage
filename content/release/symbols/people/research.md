## Current visual-story evidence

An eye 巳 pierced by a dagger 戈, an old mark of slavery / Pictograph of a line going into the eye. Relation to current meaning unclear. / 眾萌也。从古文之象。凡民之屬皆从民。 / Pictogram (象形): Later jiajie (假借) for 民 (OC *min, “the people”).
Shuowen does not have a glyph origin for this character.
Many, including Shirakawa, have claimed that it represents an eye pierced by a dagger. Sometimes explained as the original form of 盲 (OC *mraːŋ, “blind”), but the phonetics differ too much for this to be believable.
From Proto-Sino-Tibetan *r-mi(j)-n (“people, man, human being”). Cognate with Tibetan མི (mi) and Dzongkha མི (mi).
