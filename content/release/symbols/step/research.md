## Current visual-story evidence

Modern form of 歨; putting one foot 止 in front of the other / Pictograph of one foot in front of another, depicting footsteps. / 行也。从止𣥂相背。凡步之屬皆从步。 / Ideogrammic compound (會意 /会意): 止 (“foot”) + 止 (“foot”) reversed – one foot in front of the other. Compare: 夅, 舛, other characters deriving from "two feet". See also the inner component of 歲. Unrelated to 少.
