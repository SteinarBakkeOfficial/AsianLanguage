## Current visual-story evidence

Two  parallel lines; compare 一 (one) and 三 (three) / Two horizontal strokes, representing the number two. / 地之數也。从偶一。凡二之屬皆从二。 / Ideogram (指事) - two parallel strokes. Compare with 一, one stroke meaning one, 三, three strokes meaning three, and 亖 (sǐ), four strokes meaning four. Duplication of 一 (“one”).
From Proto-Sino-Tibetan *kV-ni-s. Cognate to Tibetan གཉིས (gnyis), Sikkimese ཉི (nyi), Tangut 𗍫 (*njɨ̱¹), Burmese နှစ် (hnac), and Nuosu ꑍ (nyip). See also: 次 (OC *sn̥ʰis, “second; next”), a cognate within Chinese languages.
