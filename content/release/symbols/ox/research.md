## Editorial research review — 2026-09-14

The local Oracle specimen and the current prompt are supported by independent references. CUHK describes the early form as a front-facing ox head with two curved horns and notes that ears, eyes, and nose are visible in Bronze forms. Academia Sinica likewise identifies the form as a horned cattle head and gives the original animal meaning. No competing interpretation affecting the illustration target was found, so the concise learner caption remains appropriate.
