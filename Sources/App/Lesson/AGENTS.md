# Symbol Journey guardrails

- The Symbol Journey is the primary experience for one Shared Character.
- Evolution Stages, not the former six-step rail, are the primary user-facing navigation.
- Today / Modern Endpoint is the final journey destination for selected focus tracks.
- Exact stage position must remain resumable.
- Historical Assets are source-backed/licensed or explicit Missing Historical Assets.
- Never fabricate historical glyphs or use a modern character as a historical fallback.
- Character structure is stage-aware; simple pictographs should not receive redundant panels.
- Final layout, tokens, and styling must come from the approved Fire design handoff.
