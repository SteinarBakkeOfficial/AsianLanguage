# Assets

App-native visual assets for historical forms, modern forms, icons, and typography.

For the symbol evolution experience, Origin is educational concept art, Oracle Bone/Bronze/Small Seal/Clerical are dedicated source-backed historical assets or explicit missing-asset placeholders, and Regular Script is rendered from the approved Kai font. Do not reuse a modern form as a fake historical stage.

The current 126-character ZDIC historical package remains under `content/research/` pending rights, editorial approval, and runtime packaging. It is not currently part of this bundled asset directory.

The character-evolution reference images are product targets for composition and navigation, but they are not production assets unless licensing and source quality are explicitly resolved.

## Collection artwork

`Collections/` contains the ten individual horizontal panels extracted from `Reference Pictures/Chatgpt/Collection_Design_V1.png`. These are editorial collection-cover assets, not historical glyph evidence. Their embedded titles are intentional: the full panel is the tappable collection visual, rather than a small symbol thumbnail with a separately repeated title.

The source artwork is an internal ChatGPT design reference and remains subject to final provenance and visual review before release publication.
