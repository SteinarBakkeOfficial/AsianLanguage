# AsianLanguage — Current Product Brief

## Product

AsianLanguage is an English-first, offline-first iPhone learning/reference experience about shared Chinese-character heritage across Simplified Chinese/Mandarin, Traditional Chinese usage in Taiwan and Hong Kong, Japanese Kanji, and Korean Hanja with modern Korean context.

It is not a conventional language course, flashcard app, generic dictionary, grammar curriculum, or static etymology encyclopedia. Its primary outcome is cross-language recognition:

> “I understand where this symbol came from, and now I can recognize its relationship across Chinese, Japanese, and Korean.”

The core content object is the \`Shared Character\`.

## Symbol Journey

Each Shared Character is presented as a continuous journey:

1. recognizable real-world idea or pictorial origin
2. defensible historical Evolution Stages
3. Clerical Script as the final primary historical museum stage
4. Regular Script as a separate modern standardized reference overview
5. Today / Modern Endpoint with parallel locale-specific modern forms, readings, variants, usage, and recognition recap

The historical journey is the primary information architecture. Internal Content Phases such as Character structure, Modern usage, and Summary may support the journey but must not become a competing six-button navigator.

## Root shell

The five root areas are:

- Home — resumes or introduces the next Shared Character
- Symbol — canonical owner of the active Symbol Journey
- History — generic script-period and methodology explanations
- Browse — Search, Browse All, Learned, Review later, Favorites, Saved, and collections
- More — Languages, Settings, About / Method, Account placeholder, reset, and offline information

## V1 scope

V1 targets a bundled read-only corpus of 126 characters with complete Oracle Bone, Bronze, Small Seal, and Clerical coverage. The current repository still contains the 11-record draft prototype; the 126-character ZDIC research package is local for review but is not yet bundled.

V1 also requires locale-specific modern forms/readings/examples, search, Browse collections, and the Symbol Journey. CNS11643 Kai and the selected Adobe Source Han Serif locale faces are now downloaded, bundled, and documented for the Regular Script and Used Today layers.

All four focus tracks are enabled by default. There is no user-facing \`All\` enum case.

V1 excludes grammar lessons, voice/audio, tracing, Android, sync, deep accounts, ads, and monetization. These remain longer-term or deferred concepts, not current implementation requirements.

## Content status and pilot

The current 11 records are draft prototype fixtures. They are not publication-ready. Fire, Water, Mountain, Tree, and eventually Horse form the first visual pilot direction; Horse must not be added until its content and assets are sourced.

The first design target is Fire (\`火\`). Only after the schema, asset pipeline, source process, and Symbol Journey work for 3–5 excellent pilot symbols should the corpus expand.

Historical forms must be source-backed or licensed, explicitly unavailable, or editorially omitted. Fabricated glyphs and modern forms used as historical substitutes are prohibited.
